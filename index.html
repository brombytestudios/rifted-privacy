Privacy Policy for the app “Rifted”

Android app by Brombyte Studios, package name com.rifted.game · Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short

  • Your save game stays on your device (section 2). We have no access to it. Rifted has no account and no online features.
  • Optional rewarded videos come from Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (section 3). The ad SDK connects only after you have answered the question “Personalised ads?”, and it transmits technical data such as your advertising ID.
  • Purchases of rift crystals, the starter pack and “Skip videos” go through Google Play (section 4). We receive no payment data.
  • Google processes data as the operator of Google Play and Android, independently of us (section 5).

2. Data on your device

2.1 Save game and settings

The app stores your save game in its private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It holds your progress in the game: your heroes, your items, your progress, your achievements, your rift crystals and cosmetics, the order numbers of your purchases (section 4), your settings, your choice on personalised ads and the language you chose. It contains no name, no e-mail address and no other information that identifies you.

The app processes this data only locally on your device, to provide the game to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Nothing of it is transmitted to us, and we have no access to it.

2.2 Deleting

You delete all of the app's data on your device by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage in the Android settings under Apps → Rifted → Storage → Clear storage (the exact wording depends on the device). Your progress and crystals are then lost; one-time purchases (starter pack, “Skip videos”) are restored from Google Play (section 4).

2.3 Android backup

If you have enabled backup to your Google account in Android, Android may back up the app's data (save game and settings) to your Google account automatically and restore it on a new device. This is a feature of Android and Google, controlled under Settings → Google → Backup. Google's privacy policy applies to it. We have no access to these backups.

3. Advertising: Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads

3.1 What we offer

Rifted shows only optional rewarded videos: the gold of a rift once more, a daily chest and an instant revive once per rift. There are no forced ads, no banners and no interruptions. A video starts only when you tap a button with the video symbol. If you bought “Skip videos”, you get these rewards without a video and the ad SDK does not connect to the ad servers.

3.2 Providers

The videos are mediated by Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation, technically the ironSource SDK). Unity Ads is currently the only ad network integrated. Both services are operated by Unity Technologies Inc., San Francisco (USA), including its subsidiary ironSource Ltd., Tel Aviv (Israel).

3.3 When the ad SDK runs

The ad SDK connects to the ad servers only after you have answered the question “Personalised ads?”, which appears once after your first rift – and only if you have not bought “Skip videos”. From then on it preloads videos in the background, even if you never watch one. The technical data listed in section 3.4 is therefore transmitted even if you never start a video.

3.4 Data that is transmitted

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and, derived from it, an approximate location at country or region level,
  • device and network information: manufacturer, model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier,
  • app information: package name and version,
  • your interactions with ads: video started, completed, tapped,
  • technical diagnostics of the SDK (errors, loading times),
  • according to Unity Ads, also information about in-app purchases and an SDK-internal user identifier.

With your consent this data is also used for personalised advertising, that is, for interest profiles. The SDK may also use the Android advertising services of the Privacy Sandbox (Topics, Attribution Reporting).

3.5 Purposes

Delivering ads and attributing your reward, limiting how often ads are shown, settlement with the ad network, detecting fraud and abuse, error analysis and statistics. With your consent, additionally the personalisation of ads.

3.6 Legal bases

Personalised advertising: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future in the game under Settings → “Personalised: on/off”.
Non-personalised advertising: our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.7 Transfers to third countries

Unity processes data in the USA and in Israel. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). For Israel an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR).

3.8 Retention

Retention periods are set by Unity. Details are in Unity's privacy policy for game players and app users.

3.9 Your choices

  • Withdraw consent: switch personalised ads off under Settings → “Personalised: on/off”.
  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: in the Android settings under Google → All services → Ads (on some devices Privacy → Ads). Without an advertising ID the SDK receives only a string of zeros.
  • Tap the privacy icon in a running ad to open the privacy options of Unity Ads.
  • Request access or deletion from Unity through the form named in Unity's privacy policy linked above. We ourselves store none of this advertising data and therefore can neither view nor delete it.

4. Purchases through Google Play

Rift crystals, the starter pack and “Skip videos” are bought through Google Play's billing system. Crystals buy cosmetics only (skins, wings, companions, weapon skins). Google handles the payment (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). We receive no payment data, no name and no address.

From Google Play the app receives only the product bought, the order or transaction number, the time and the state of the purchase. The order number is stored in your save game so that no purchase is credited twice. After a reinstall, Google Play tells the app which one-time purchases (starter pack, “Skip videos”) you have, so that they are restored. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Purchase reports to Unity: The purchasing library in the app (Unity In-App Purchasing by Unity Technologies Inc., USA) also reports every purchase to Unity: purchase started, paid, failed and completed. A report contains the product with price and currency, the Google Play receipt (order number, purchase token and signature), device model, Android version, language settings, app version, install time, and a session and an installation identifier from Unity. Unity receives your IP address in the process. Unity collects this telemetry for its purchasing service; the built-in version of the library cannot switch it off. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in reliable purchase processing (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For the transfer to the USA, section 3.7 applies.

Your purchase history and refunds are managed by Google in your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies to them.

5. Google Play and Android

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes the data needed for this, for example your Google account, your device and the time of the download. Google Play may also collect crash and performance data of the app (Android Vitals) through your Google account if you have allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data. Google does this as the operator of Google Play and Android (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, for users in the EEA). We have no influence over this; Google's privacy policy applies. From Google we receive at most aggregated statistics without any reference to individual persons. The app itself uses no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
