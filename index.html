Privacy Policy for the app “Rifted”

Android app by Brombyte Studios, package name com.rifted.game · Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short

  • We collect no personal data and share none. Rifted has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no account and no online features.
  • The app has no network access. It does not have Android's internet permission and cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Your save game stays on your device (section 2). We have no access to it.
  • Google processes data as the operator of Google Play and Android, independently of us (section 3).

2. Data on your device

2.1 Save game and settings

The app stores your save game in its private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It holds your progress in the game: your heroes, your items, your progress, your achievements, your settings and the language you chose. It contains no name, no e-mail address and no other information that identifies you.

The app processes this data only locally on your device, to provide the game to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Nothing of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no access to it.

2.2 Deleting

You delete all of the app's data on your device by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage in the Android settings under Apps → Rifted → Storage → Clear storage (the exact wording depends on the device). Your progress is then lost.

2.3 Android backup

If you have enabled backup to your Google account in Android, Android may back up the app's data (save game and settings) to your Google account automatically and restore it on a new device. This is a feature of Android and Google, controlled under Settings → Google → Backup. Google's privacy policy applies to it. We have no access to these backups.

3. Google Play and Android

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes the data needed for this, for example your Google account, your device and the time of the download. Google Play may also collect crash and performance data of the app (Android Vitals) through your Google account if you have allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data. Google does this as the operator of Google Play and Android (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, for users in the EEA). We have no influence over this; Google's privacy policy applies. From Google we receive at most aggregated statistics without any reference to individual persons. The app itself uses no analytics or crash-reporting services.

4. App permissions

The app does not ask you for any permission. In particular it has no permission for internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, notifications or access to photos and files.

5. E-mails to us

If you write to us by e-mail, we use your e-mail address and the content of your message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete the message once your request is settled and no statutory retention obligation applies.

6. Children and young people

The app is not directed at children under 16. Since the app collects no personal data, it does not collect any data from children either.

7. Your rights

Towards us you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example with the authority responsible for us: the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

Because the app transmits no data to us, we do not hold any data about you from the app. You delete the data on your device yourself (section 2.2). Requests about Google Play and Android go to Google (section 3). Feel free to write to us anyway, and we will help you along.

8. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address. This version is dated 1 November 2026.
